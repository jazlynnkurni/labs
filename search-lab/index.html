One search over everything you have, that tells you what it thinks you meant.

The bar reads the question before it answers it. Say a person, a time, a kind of thing or a topic in any order and it shows that reading back as chips, then ranks docs, mail, meetings, files and open tabs against it. The chips are the control: remove one and the results move.

Everything behind it is a stand-in list in data.js. Real connectors replace the list; the bar does not change.
