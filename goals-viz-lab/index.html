Slide 8, Depop’s two processes

Same deck type and colours. Discovery, then Deal, joined by making an offer.

1 · Venn

Discovery and Deal as two overlapping sets. Making an offer is the overlap, the one moment that belongs to both.

After Drew Conway’s data science Venn diagram, where the overlap carries the point. KDnuggets

2 · Bowtie

Discovery narrows down to one piece, the offer is the knot where the buyer commits, and Deal opens out into price, payment and shipping.

After Winning by Design’s bowtie, with the commit point as the knot. Winning by Design

3 · Journey

Five real Depop screens in order, with Discovery and Deal bracketed above and each point captioned under its screen. The offer screen is the hinge, ringed in plum.

After NN/g’s journey map: phases over actions. NN/g

4 · Metro

Two lines with the points as stations, and making an offer as the interchange where you change from Discovery to Deal.

After Harry Beck’s 1933 Tube diagram. TfL

5 · Value chain

Each process is a chevron in a chain, and the offer is the link between them, drawn as the deck’s own dark block arrow.

After Michael Porter’s value chain. HBS ISC
