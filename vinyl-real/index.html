Real vinyl · five renderings

A record that reads as a record.

What a pressed record actually looks like: almost black, a satin sheen rather than gloss, the grooves invisible except as two soft fans of light opposite each other that stay fixed while the disc turns, a dead-wax ring, a paper label with a bright embossed rim, a tiny spindle hole. Five takes on that below, all drawn in canvas from a height field or a shading model, no images. Hover to spin. The big one at the bottom is whichever you pick, at size.
