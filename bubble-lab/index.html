Bubble, the remote for a Glinda bubble over New York

iOS 26 Liquid Glass, SwiftUI: every control is glass over a pale Prussian sky, capsules and continuous corners, floating tab bar, one Prussian glass primary.

One system, five directions. Each opens as a row of five phone screens: pair, home, fly, route, land. The controls work: drag the dial, the stick, the sliders, draw a route. White and Prussian blue only. Pick one and the rest get built out in that language.

01

Instrument

A flight instrument in white. Hairline dial for heading, a vertical scale for altitude, large light numerals. Blue is the needle and nothing else.
02

Glass

The bubble is the control. One soft sphere you push from inside to steer; words in lowercase, very few of them. The quietest of the five.
03

Transit

New York wayfinding. Station bullets, a route strip with stops, one full-width GO. Reads like the system map and feels like it belongs to the city.
04

Remote

A physical remote drawn in white plastic. D-pad, a long altitude pill, an e-ink readout strip, a hold-to-land ring. Strict 8-point grid.
05

Map

The map is the screen. A hairline New York, the bubble as a blue dot, and you draw where it goes with a finger; a bottom sheet holds the numbers.
