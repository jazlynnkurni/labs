Five ways the hero circles say they can move

Your eight swatches and your formation in each. Same wall, same discs; only how they answer the hand changes.

  1. 1 · Albers, colour answersThey lean toward your hand. Drag one across the others and every overlap it touches recolours. It springs home.
  2. 2 · Kandinsky, several circlesDark ground, slow breath, a turning ring under the hand. Throw one; it knocks the others and they drift home.
  3. 3 · Matisse, paper cut-outsHover lifts a circle off the wall on its shadow. Carry it; it flexes with speed and stays where you drop it.
  4. 4 · Common fateNo clicking. They part around your hand like a shoal, nudge each other, and ease back.
  5. 5 · LiquidDrops that run together. Pull one and it stretches out of the others before it lets go.
