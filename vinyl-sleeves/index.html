Sleeve systems · the house decides, the logo visits

Five sleeves that make seven logos one series.

Seven logos in seven voices will never sit together on their own. A record label solves this with a house sleeve: the type, the paper and the layout are the brand, and whatever the artist brings is held in a fixed place at a fixed size, usually in one ink. Each row below is one house, in the site's own materials: paper, grain, Jakarta labels, Helvetica numbers, hair rules, the eight riso tints, sage and mauve. Hover any sleeve.
