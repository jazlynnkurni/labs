The word wall as paper

The page is the sheet and it stays white; newsprint is what is torn from it or under it.

  1. 1 · Ransom cut-outsEvery letter its own torn scrap, mixed papers and type, lifting on hover.
  2. 2 · Torn stripsEach word one strip ripped from a newspaper, white fibres at the tear.
  3. 3 · Accordion foldThe white page folded along every cell seam; the folds breathe, hover flattens a row.
  4. 4 · Torn through the pageEach letter a hole torn in the page, a newspaper underneath.
  5. 5 · Peeling flapsEach letter a flap of the page that peels from its corner on hover.
  6. 6 · Peel, signaturePurple and green under the flaps; JAZLYNN hides a message, every other word one small thing, and one flap a visit is gold.
