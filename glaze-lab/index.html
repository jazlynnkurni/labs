The one glass action from design.how/systems/glaze, recorded frame by frame and rebuilt. Press takes 50 ms: scale to .99, the top sheen goes to 0, the dark core deepens from .32 to .44, the gloss pill drops to 60%. Release takes 200 ms on ease-out (.2, .8, .2, 1). Only scale and the gloss opacity ease; the gradient alphas snap. Hover only lifts the sheen from .12 to .16, and snaps too. The five below keep that timing and change one thing each.

4Handoff

Glaze's rule is glass only on the one action you press next. So when you press it, the glass should leave. Send it goes flat and reads Sent, and in 160 ms the glass appears on the next action in the stack. The material is the cursor of intent.
