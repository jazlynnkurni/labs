## Slide 1 · Context

**Headline:** Business Context, Problem Space, & Use Case

**On the slide:** a credit-based fitness marketplace connecting members with studios looking to fill unused capacity. The business model, the business challenge, the user journey (Discover → Book → Attend or cancel), and Abigail's card.

**What ClassPass is (from my notes).** A subscription-based fitness and wellness marketplace. Members book classes and appointments across many studios using credits. It connects people who want flexibility and variety with studios that want to fill unused capacity. ClassPass earns from subscriptions and pays partners per booking.

**How the supply side works (from my notes).** The Mindbody integration means the studio already has you when you arrive. What used to be ten minutes of paperwork is now automated. SmartRate prices spots dynamically by popularity, location and time. SmartTools uses real-time data to predict empty spots and adjust rates to fill them. SmartSpot looks at historic booking patterns and only lists spots unlikely to fill on their own, which protects the studio's direct clients.

**The tension.** ClassPass has to balance member flexibility and perceived credit value against partner revenue and class availability. I'll evaluate how well discovery and the credit system communicate value while encouraging sustainable booking.

**Who uses it (from my notes).** A typical member is 25 to 45. They have tried boutique fitness but aren't loyal to one brand. They seek variety and like trying different workouts and wellness experiences each month.

**The three member interactions (from my notes).** Buying credits, booking a class, and the social side, where you see friends book. I'm a member who mostly just wants to get my workouts done, so I follow one path: ordering a class, then either going to it or cancelling it.

**Abigail.** She does fitness and food marketing in NYC. She's in her early 20s and wants to be fit and social. She's never used ClassPass, but a friend told her great things. She books a class for Saturday. Then she realises it clashes with a restaurant reservation she has with that friend, and she needs to cancel. What does she have to do?

**Why Home and Profile.** I assume these are the two pages with the most daily use, since everyone lands on Home. Home drives class discovery and booking. Profile holds account and subscription management. My notes say ClassPass facilitated over 62 million reservations in 2025, so even small gains in the booking journey add up. *(Check this figure against a source before presenting.)*

**Framing note.** This is a scenario-based critique, informed by first-hand use and the screens I captured. It isn't user research.
