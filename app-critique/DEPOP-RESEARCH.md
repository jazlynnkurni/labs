## Praise people repeat

- App Store rating 4.8 from 1.1M US ratings (537k UK), so complaints are a loud minority.
- Trustpilot praise: easy app and liked layout, wide selection, fair prices, fast refunds for not-as-described items.
- Offers are popular: over 40% of items bought via offers, average 23% discount, 62M offers in 2023 (Depop newsroom, Jan 2024): https://news.depop.com/depop-community-embraces-offers-as-suite-of-negotiation-tools-expands/
- One App Store reviewer calls the filters useful and the vintage selection strong.
