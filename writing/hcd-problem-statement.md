# HCD problem statement (Week 5 form: For / Who need to / Design)

**For:** young adults aged 21 to 28 in New York who use the subway for discretionary trips, such as dinner, friends and museums, rather than a daily commute.

**Who need to:** know that a trip has broken while they still have an alternative. Today, skip-stop changes and wrong travel estimates reach them only once they are on the train, past the point of choice. Each failed journey costs an evening or a neighbourhood, and at six dollars a day they quietly stop going and call it preference.

**Design:** a way to keep a planned trip trustworthy from decision to arrival.
