# Journal: Music in ten years

In ten years I expect AI to hold the middle of music and humans to hold the ends. The middle is arrangement, mixing, the fourth verse nobody wanted to write, the background track for an advert or a game. Models will do that work quickly and cheaply, and most listeners will not ask who made it. The ends are the first idea and the room. Someone still has to want a song to exist, and someone still has to stand in front of people and play it, because a performance is partly the risk that it goes wrong.

I concede this leaves working musicians in the middle with less to do, and that worries me more than the question of real music ever did. My own feeling is that the recorded song becomes cheap and the live one becomes dear. I am sad for the studio musician and glad for the small venue.
