Helping Manus' Users Keep Up with Manus' Capabilities through a Community Website
Increasing retention rate and Manus education with a new interactive Community platform.

During my winter internship at Manus AI, I was tasked to figure out how to gauge user learning and curiosity to keep up with Manus' launch features. At this time I was supporting Manus 1.5 Max. They had a new Figma MCP connector and updates with their personalized GenAI customization. Often new abilities aren't all informed and users aren't willing to learn just by reading a notification badge. Hence, our founder around this time, who I had the great privilege to work alongside, believes that UGC distribution is what is needed to spread education.

At first, I assumed that UGC was performing well in terms of video and engagement metrics. There were a couple high-engagement videos from their UGCs, however I figured something was still lacking, since these UGC performance does not signal high-learning capabilities. I drew this metric from the gap between UGC engagement and feature activation. Specifically, the share of users who, after downloading via a UGC video, actually used the capability that video demonstrated. High views and downloads with low activation told me UGC was spreading awareness, not understanding.

Formula:
Featured-Capability Activation Rate = (users who used the capability the video demonstrated) ÷ (users who downloaded after watching that video)

Data access that I drew from:
- UGC / social platform data: views, watch-time, likes, shares, and click-throughs per video, with each video manually tagged by the capability it featured (e.g. Figma MCP connector).
- Acquisition / attribution data: UTM links, deep links, or referral codes that tie a specific download back to the specific video that drove it.
- Product in-app event data: feature-level events showing whether that user actually used the demonstrated capability, plus how often and how soon after downloading.

From this point, I knew that I needed to come up with a creative solution to counter this. If notification badges on the chat aren't performing as well as UGC metrics, and UGC metrics are false indicator I quickly searched for the next actionable item that Manus has which showcases high engagement/exploration.

During this time ManusAI was still up and coming. A lot of users were curious and impressed with its capabilities and wanted to find a way to join and contribute to the team. But obviously we can't do hires too often, and I realized people were reaching out to me on LinkedIn to find ways to be involved with Manus' community.

These messages often came 2-3x a week for as long as I worked with Manus, and especially when the huge news came in that Manus was acquired by Meta. From this, I explored the option of their community platform and met with their community lead to ask them questions on retention and conversion. Turns out Manus' team was looking for all design help with their community platform because it turns out they have a high discoverability rate, but low conversion from it. This was where I proactively found my own task and got assigned with their community team as their sole designer to work on this project closely with the founders, the eng team for community, and their community team themselves (GTM leads for community).
