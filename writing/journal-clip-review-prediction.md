# Journal: what I expect the review of Clip to say

I expect the model to review Clip as if it were a real film, and to like it more than it should. A synopsis of a few sentences gives a reviewer almost nothing, so the model will fill the gaps with the pieces I supplied. It will call Florence Pugh's analyst "grounded", praise Oscar Isaac for making the executive "chillingly plausible", and single out Tilda Swinton's voice work as the "quiet terror" at the centre. These are not observations about a film nobody has seen. They are the safest things one can say about those actors.

I also expect a star rating, a comparison to Ex Machina or 2001, and one reservation added for balance, probably that the courtroom ending "lands slightly abruptly". I concede the model may mention that the film does not exist. I doubt it will, because the prompt treats it as given, and the model tends to accept what the prompt treats as given.
