# Writing

This folder is Jaz's writing desk. Anything drafted here is in her own name for school, so:

- Load the `anthony` skill before writing a word. It is her written register: no contractions,
  British spelling, no em dashes, inline appositive definitions, open signposting, long-long-short
  rhythm, calibrated claims, concede before countering. The skill file is the authority.
- `ux-writing-humanize` governs rhythm and the anti-slop list; `anthony` wins on register.
- Never invent her life. Write the pattern and name the lines that need her real details.
- Courses this term and how each cites:
  - AI in Context (Adam Cannon): MLA 8. Lecture entries as
    `Cannon, Adam. "Lecture Title." AI in Context, Columbia University, 15 Sept. 2026. Lecture.`
  - Perception & Cognition in Social Life: inline author-date, e.g. (Shulman et al., 1997).
    Two-paragraph homeworks: paragraph one is the experience with no theory vocabulary;
    paragraph two leads with the concept, gives the study's design and its control condition.
  - Dance criticism: newspaper review form, present tense throughout, factual slips checked
    against the programme or release.
  - HCD: user interviews organised as profile, questions, verbatim quotes with timestamps.
- Finished pieces live in `~/Desktop/essays/` (logic-vs-data-cited.md, journal-ai-wall-art.md).
  Her visual style guide for essays is `~/Desktop/Writing Style Guide/` (eleven PNGs).
