# Journal: do the models have personalities?

Yes, they are distinguishable, though less by what they say than by how they open. Claude, which I use in the terminal for hours, tends to restate what it thinks I asked and hedge before it answers. ChatGPT answers first and lists second. Gemini, which wrote the notes for an interview I recorded this month, summarised a 23-year-old in the third person as "they" and never once sounded like anyone. Some of this is behaviour, because the companies train for a tone and it shows in the first sentence every time. Some of it is me. I treat the one I sit with all day as a colleague and the others as tools, and a colleague gets a personality whether it has one or not. Could I tell them apart blind? On a long task, probably, from the hedging. On a one-line question, I would be guessing.
