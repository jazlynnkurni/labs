# Journal: do I talk to AI differently?

I talk to AI the way I text, and I talk to people the way I write. With Claude I type in lowercase, skip the greeting, leave the typos, and say things like "150 words man cmon" or "its too AI" when a draft misses. I would never send an editor that. I would soften it, thank them for the attempt, and explain. With the model I do not, because none of that softening does anything. There is no face to read and nothing to repair afterwards, so every word of social maintenance is wasted, and I drop it. What is left is blunter, but also more honest. I tell it exactly what is wrong, which is something I rarely manage with a person on the first try. The one thing I keep is the imperative. "Help me," "make this," "look at that." I still address it as someone. I have just stopped being polite to it.
