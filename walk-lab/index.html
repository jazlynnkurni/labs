Walk lab

One man, one briefcase, five ways.

The walk underneath is the same on every page: hip, knee and ankle follow published gait-lab curves, the planted foot is held still in the world so nothing slides, and the case is a pendulum on the wrist. Only the rendering changes.

  1. 01
    Long exposureSixteen moments inside an open shutter. The swinging foot and the case smear, the planted foot stays sharp, and a trail refuses to fade.
  2. 02
    DreamSix of him in a line, one stride apart, whoever leaves rejoins at the back. Three colourways to switch between: riso red, nocturne, jade.
  3. 02a
    Dream, riso redThe man in riso red on cream, the case in black, blue echoes.
  4. 02b
    Dream, nocturneDark ground. He glows pale instead of staining, the case electric orange.
  5. 02c
    Dream, jadeDeep jade man, vermilion case, sage echoes on mint.
  6. 03
    FiguresHe is made of his own numbers: joint angles, phase and speed, streamed through a grid he walks across. Readouts hang off the joints.
  7. 04
    LinesHatch lines where the body is, a hairline skeleton, and the curves each joint draws as he walks, left on the paper.
  8. 06
    Out of Office, footerThe line walking through a site footer: brand, four link columns, legal row, socials, and the three colour dots as the only control.
  9. 07
    ThreeJust the animation: three of him across the window, each with his shadow following. Also on its own port, 6130.
  10. 05
    ChronophotographEight exposures a stride on one plate, Marey's way, so the row of copies from the reference GIF builds itself in front of you.

Space pauses. The panel sets tempo (seconds per stride, 1.08 is the clip), height, lean and the speed of time. check.html is the gait strip used to verify the cycle.
