Pressings · seven discs, glass

Every record its own pressing.

Real grooves, drawn as rings with a spiral lead-in and the gaps between tracks, on a disc that is glass rather than vinyl: light refracts across the grooves as it turns, with a soft caustic along the rim. Hover a sleeve and the record slides out and spins up. Seven pressing styles, one per project, the way a label would press a special edition.
