Liquid, as a component on its own

Seven drops in your swatches, centred. The same drops and the same drag in each; only the liquid changes.

  1. 0 · The componentThe liquid from the disc lab, alone in the middle of the page.
  2. 1 · MercuryLiquid metal on night, lit from its own surface, your colours as a tint.
  3. 2 · Ink in waterWatercolour drops: soft bleeding edges, pigment pooling at the rim, heavy grain.
  4. 3 · Lava lampThey rise, sink, meet and part on their own; your hand is heat.
  5. 4 · GooThick and shiny; pulling one draws a long string that thins and draws back.
  6. 5 · Glass lensClear drops on your words, bending the type behind them.
