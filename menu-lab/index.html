Menu lab

Five places a menu could be when you are not looking for it.

All liquid glass, all springs. Each one hides the same four options and answers the same question differently: where do they live, and how do they arrive.

  1. 01
    BeadA drop in the top right corner. Reach it and the options spring out of it to the left, nearest first, while the bead leans into the release.
  2. 02
    PourA drop hangs from the top edge. Reach it and it pours sideways into a bar, overshooting its width, the labels surfacing from the centre outward. A bead detaches and falls.
  3. 03
    OrbitA disc in the corner. Four lenses swing out around it on an arc, each an angular spring that overshoots its bearing and swings back.
  4. 04
    TideA film of glass along the top edge. It bulges up to meet the pointer like a meniscus and the nearest label surfaces in the bulge. Click and the whole tide comes in.
  5. 05
    SplitOne drop divides. Four droplets pull out of it, stretching liquid necks, each running its own arc to a place along the top, where it clears into glass.

The bead's circle is a shader orb; pick which in the orb lab. Hover opens, leaving closes, click pins, Escape closes. Every motion is a damped spring in spring.js, so it can be retargeted mid-flight without a jump.
